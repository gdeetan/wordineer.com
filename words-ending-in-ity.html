About words ending in -ITY

The "-ity" suffix nominalises adjectives into abstract nouns of quality. "Able" becomes "ability," "real" becomes "reality," "active" becomes "activity." The resulting nouns name the quality in the abstract, making them essential vocabulary for discussing ideas, traits, and conditions across academic and professional writing.

Part of speech: Abstract nouns expressing a quality or condition (ability, reality, creativity). The suffix derives from Latin "-itas" and its French form "-ité." It almost always pairs with an adjective base.

CityCommunityQualitySecurityUniversityAbilityAccountabilityActivityAudacityAuthenticityAuthorityAvailability

Using -ITY words in writing and word games

"ITY" words tend to be long and therefore high-scoring in word games. "CITY" and "PITY" are short exceptions, but "QUALITY," "GRAVITY," and "CLARITY" can anchor a strong board position.
