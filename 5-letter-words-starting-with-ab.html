Frequently asked questions

How many 5-letter words start with AB?

This list has 41 five-letter words starting with AB that have definitions. 14 are common everyday words. Wordle's answer list includes 6 of them.

What are the best AB words for Wordle?

Abate first. Abide if I is untested. Abode when O is the last vowel. Skip creat-style non-answers; stay on this list’s Wordle words.

Abate already used my A and E. Next?

If the second A missed, abide or abode. If E hit, abyss or abhor depending on whether you need Y or R.
