      8-letter words starting with K

      This page brings together 120 curated eight-letter words that begin with K, each paired with a definition, part of speech, and difficulty level. That makes it useful as both a fast puzzle reference and a longer study page for vocabulary building. Eight-letter words are long enough to feel specific and expressive, but still compact enough to scan quickly when you are checking answer shapes, preparing class materials, or narrowing down a word-game guess.

      The K section is especially useful because it combines highly familiar everyday words with a deeper layer of scientific, geographic, technical, and literary vocabulary. Familiar entries such as keyboard, kindness, kangaroo, keystone, and kilogram are practical and memorable, while words like kohlrabi, klystron, kuroshio, kinkajou, and kwakiutl give the page more depth for advanced study and competitive play.

Words such as keyboard, kindness, knockout, kangaroo, and keystone are clear and reusable, while less obvious items like kohlrabi, klystron, kinkajou, and kuroshio help widen your search space when a puzzle is less predictable.
