    These tools do one thing: help you find the right name, whether for your business or your story.

    Business names have to work in the real world; fiction names just have to fit the story. It's the first thing your customers see. The beauty of fiction is the creativity it enables; you create the plot, then think of names for it. The most challenging part is thinking of names, and that is where these tools come into play.

    All 23 generators here are designed to help with names for entertainment, imagination, and even branding. So use these tools differently than the ones on the Name Generators page. For example, historical fiction set in 1920s Paris would require real French names, whereas fantasy set in a fictional kingdom would not.

    Fantasy and fictional character names

    More generators here cover fantasy and fictional characters than any other category. Much Fantasy fiction is of the High Fantasy, Urban Fantasy, or Paranormal Romance varieties and is highly dependent upon how believable the invented world is.

    Names in a work of Fantasy are often a major part of establishing a fictional setting or world. This means that while a character named "Bob" would be completely out of place in most Fantasy worlds, a completely novel name could draw the reader into the story if it sounds as though it could occur in that particular fictional setting.

    These generators for fantasy names tend to focus on specific scenarios or character types and generate names suited to those contexts rather than to other characters or stories.

    So, for example, an elf's name will often have an ethereal quality and sound especially when spoken, while a villain will have a name that sounds especially evil and conveys a sense of threat even when read individually. Barbarian names are generally very primal and forceful, and would look good sewn onto the fur of a barbarian's cloak. (And note that Harry Potter names are specifically tuned to the Potterverse, rather than to "British"-style names in general. "Sounds British" is not the same as "sounds like it belongs in the Hogwarts universe").

    Keep in mind there's no right or wrong here. These tools are merely sparks to help writers get into the right mindset, complete a prompt or passage, and explore other areas of a writer's creative process that inspire. These are just a few spark generators that can help writers of any genre, whether they're stuck for ideas or have too many.

    Naming your pets

    Other owners name their pets after their personalities, which reveal themselves after a few weeks. A big tough dog might be named "Scout" or "Chaos" by its owners. The generators on this page are weighted towards these sorts of names, which are short, cute, very memorable, and even work well for very large animals as well as for very small animals like hamsters.

    This generator works because it's drawing from a pool of names that real pet owners have actually used. For pets, we look for names that are simple enough to yell out across a crowded room or park, but that also sound nice and have the right amount of personality and quirk for the individual animal. Typically, that means a single word that is familiar, yet not too common. The names here will work for any small animal, from dogs and cats to fish, snakes, and lizards.

    Band names, musician names, and team names

    Band, musician, team, corporation, startup, and club names can have a big impact. A band name, for instance, must be searchable, memorable, distinguishable from other bands, and look good in a Spotify playlist. If you choose a name that is too generic, it will get lost amongst the many other bands with similar names. If you choose a name that is too obscure, it will be impossible for people to remember how you spelled it. In addition, there is the big risk of an inside joke going wrong. Something that makes the drummer of a band laugh in rehearsal may be terrible in front of an audience.

    For band names, I run them through the band name generator, and the output is patched together with all sorts of name ideas, both real and completely fictional. A band name generator is used to come up with names that would fit your group. The band's name helps form your online presence, so you have to choose wisely. The need to come up with band names is an additional barrier to entry for would-be musicians creating and performing music. And for named solo artists, there are additional generators for Solo Project Names. (Note that naming your band is distinct from naming yourself as a solo artist, and each has its own requirements and restrictions).

    Business names and branding

    Naming a startup can be simple but tedious. The challenge here is to come up with ideas for a good name — something that's easy to remember, brandable, and makes a good first impression. Unique enough that no other company owns the name, and it can be trademarked.

    Also, the choice of name determines whether the desired domain names can be purchased. As a result, the business name generator was designed to generate names that are as suitable as possible for companies and, in particular, are easy to pronounce, remember, write, and add to business cards.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the difference between this resource and the Name Generators hub?

    The Name Generators Hub is on the opposite side of the fence from the Hub with Real-World Names from Specific Countries & Traditions. The Names from Specific Countries & Traditions Hub contains real names of people from around the world. The Name Generators Hub contains everything you need for invented, fictional, and creative purposes when a name from real life is not needed or is not suitable. Here you will find names for Fantasy characters, pets, bands, and business branding.

    Are all these generators free?

    Yes, all 23 generators are totally free. No subscription required. No ads in the tool. And no "premium" version that you'd have to pay for. You can use as many of the free name generators as you like, any time you like.

    Can I use generated names commercially?

    Generated names are fine to use in published books, commercial video games, on Shopify sites, for bands, and more. You can even use Wordineer to name your next startup, then check whether it's available for trademarking using the business name generator on this site.

    Which generator should I use for a D&D character?

    A Human Fighter would likely use the Barbarian or Hero generator first. An Elf Wizard (e.g,. a bard or sorcerer) would likely use the Elf or Fantasy generators first. And, naturally, a Villain would use the Villain generator. The Character generator serves as a catch-all, providing a wide array of names for any number of characters. Most D&D players generate a few names and pick the one that best fits their character's personality and backstory.

    How does the business name generator work?

    Wordineer's business name generator has been crafted to produce the most memorable, trademarkable names. Most startups stick to short, pronounceable words, and as such, the generator produces a range of names, descriptive or evocative, mixed in one batch. There will be some literal names mixed in with more poetic alternatives.
