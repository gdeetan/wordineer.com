Frequently asked questions

How many 5-letter words start with AN?

This list has 39 five-letter words starting with AN that have definitions. 10 are common everyday words. Wordle's answer list includes 8 of them.

What are the best AN words for Wordle?

Anger first for E, G, and R. Angst if you need S and T. Angry is the guess after G and R are already placed.

Angel versus angle?

Neither is a good first probe after AN. Start with anger. Then angel or angle once E and G are known.
