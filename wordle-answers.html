How this archive is compiled

Each row is a publicly reported daily Wordle: calendar date, the five-letter answer, and the official share number (days since June 19, 2021). CIGAR is Wordle 0. Today's answer is withheld so the page stays a reference, not a spoiler farm.

This is not the New York Times' official list, and it is not affiliated with NYT Games. After the 2022 acquisition the Times removed a small number of upcoming words. Third-party archives can disagree on a handful of early dates; we use the share-number convention and keep a changelog when a row is corrected.

Need a next guess, not a history lesson? Use the Wordle Helper. For openers, see the entropy ranking of starting words.

Position matters. The most common letter in each slot is S _ A E E (S first, O second, A third, E fourth, E last). That is why CRANE, SLATE, and STARE keep showing up as openers — they hit this distribution, not because any one of them is magic. See the starting-word ranking for the entropy numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include today's Wordle answer?

No. The table and the lookup both hide today's answer. Yesterday and earlier are listed so you can check a streak argument or a shared grid without spoiling a puzzle you have not played.

What is the puzzle number?

It matches the number in a Wordle share grid — "Wordle 1884." That number is days since June 19, 2021. CIGAR is Wordle 0. Some sites count CIGAR as #1; we follow the share text so a screenshot lines up with this table.

Has Wordle started repeating answers?

Yes. 21 words in this archive appear twice. Recycling shows up in 2026 — CIGAR returned as #1689 on February 2. The lookup reports both dates when a word has been reused.

How many original-list words are still unused?

499 of the original 2,315-word pool have not appeared yet. NYT removed a small number of upcoming answers after buying Wordle, so a few remaining words may never run. Download the unused CSV to see the full set.

Can I use this data in an article or project?

Yes. The dataset is CC BY 4.0 — free to use with attribution to Wordineer (wordineer.com/wordle-answers/). The CSV and JSON are in the cite box above.

Is this affiliated with the New York Times?

No. This is an independent reference compiled from publicly reported daily answers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the New York Times.

Where should I go if I want a solver?

Use the Wordle Helper. It ranks the next guess by Shannon entropy. This page is the historical record, not a live solver.
