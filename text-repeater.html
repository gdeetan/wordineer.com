How it works

1
Enter your text

Type or paste the word, phrase, or sentence you want to duplicate into the input box.

2
Set count and separator

Choose how many repetitions you need (up to 500) and pick a separator — newline, space, comma, or none.

3
Copy the result

Hit Generate, then copy the output to your clipboard with one click and paste it wherever you need it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a text repeater?

A text repeater duplicates a word, phrase, or block of text a chosen number of times and joins the copies with a separator such as a newline, space, or comma.

How many times can I repeat text?

You can repeat text up to 500 times. Enter any whole number from 1 to 500 in the repeat count field.

What separators are available?

You can choose a newline (each repetition on its own line), a space, a comma, or no separator at all. You can also type any custom separator directly into the separator field.

Can I use this to fill placeholder content?

Yes. Designers and developers often use a text repeater to generate filler content for mockups, wireframes, or test inputs without installing any software.

Who uses this tool

Developers & QA testers
Quickly generate repetitive input strings for testing form validation, character limits, and stress-testing text fields.
Designers & content creators
Fill placeholder sections in mockups or wireframes with repeated text to simulate real content before copywriting is ready.
Students & educators
Create writing practice sheets, vocabulary drills, or repeated sentence exercises without tedious manual copy-pasting.
