How it works

1
Type your text

Enter any word, phrase, or sentence in the input box. The tool converts it as you type — no button needed.

2
Characters are mapped & reversed

Each character is replaced with its Unicode upside-down equivalent, then the whole string is reversed so it reads naturally when rotated 180°.

3
Copy & paste anywhere

Hit Copy and paste your flipped text into social media, messages, emails, or anywhere that accepts Unicode text.

Frequently asked questions

How does the upside down text generator work?

It maps each character to a Unicode equivalent that looks like the upside-down version, then reverses the string so the result reads naturally when physically rotated 180°.

Can I paste upside down text into social media?

Yes. The output uses standard Unicode characters supported on all major platforms including Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Discord.

Does it work for all letters?

It covers a–z, A–Z, 0–9, and common punctuation. A handful of uppercase letters have no distinct upside-down glyph and are mapped to their closest Unicode approximation.

Is the upside down text generator free?

Completely free with no sign-up required. Conversion happens in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
