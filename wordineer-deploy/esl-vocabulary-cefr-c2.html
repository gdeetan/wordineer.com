debacle

C2

noun · /dəˈbɑkəl/

A complete and shameful failure

“The launch turned into a public debacle.”

esoteric

C2

adjective · /ˌɛsəˈtɛrɪk/

“His research covers esoteric parts of physics.”

adjective · /ɪˈɡridʒəs/

cognizant

panacea

noun · /ˌpænəˈsiə/

erudite

C2

sanguine

Hopeful and positive about the future

“She remains sanguine about the election.”

maelstrom

A situation of great confusion or trouble

“The country fell into a maelstrom of protest.”
