About words ending in -TION

The "-tion" suffix entered English mainly through Latin and French. It signals a noun of action or result: something that was done (creation), is happening (rotation), or came about (solution). Because so much formal and academic writing leans on these nouns, recognising "-tion" words unlocks comprehension across law, medicine, science, and politics.

Part of speech: Overwhelmingly nouns. The "-tion" suffix converts verbs and adjectives into abstract nouns of action, process, or result (act → action, describe → description).

Using -TION words in writing and word games

"-TION" words score high in Scrabble because T, I, O, and N are all common tiles. Long "-tion" words (like "station" or "fraction") can land on bonus squares and clear a crowded rack at the same time.
