How to play

  1. Pick a category — Animals, Geography, Science, Food, or General Words.
  2. Guess letters — click a letter button or press a key on your keyboard. Correct guesses fill in the blanks. Wrong guesses add a body part to the gallows.
  3. 6 wrong guesses — that's your limit. Fill in all the letters before then and you win.
  4. New Game — click "New Game" at any time to start over with a fresh word from the same category.

Tips for winning

Start with the most common letters in English: E, T, A, O, I, N, S, H, R. They appear in the majority of words and give you the fastest picture of the word's shape. Save less common letters (Q, X, Z, J) for when you have enough context to guess.

Watch for short words — a 3-letter word with a blank in the middle is often the, and, or for. A 4-letter word ending in a blank after three revealed letters usually ends in S, D, or R.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Hangman?

A secret word is chosen from the selected category and shown as blank tiles. Click or type letters to guess. Correct guesses reveal the letter in every position it appears. Wrong guesses add a part to the stick figure. You have 6 wrong guesses before the game ends — fill in all letters before then and you win.

Can I use my physical keyboard?

Yes. Press any letter key on your keyboard to guess that letter. The on-screen keyboard mirrors the state of physical key presses, so you can mix both. This works on desktop and laptop computers; touch devices use the on-screen buttons.

Is this good for classroom use?

Yes. The game runs in any browser, requires no login, and works on Chromebooks, iPads, and phones. Pull it up on a projector for whole-class play or let students play individually. The Animals, Science, and Geography categories map directly to common K–8 vocabulary topics.

What categories are available?

Five categories: Animals (mammals, birds, sea creatures, reptiles), Geography (countries, continents, world features), Science (biology, chemistry, physics, earth science terms), Food (fruits, vegetables, dishes, ingredients), and General Words (a broad vocabulary mix from easy to challenging).

Who plays Hangman?

Teachers
Use hangman as a quick warm-up, vocabulary review, or end-of-lesson reward. The Science and Geography categories line up with standard curriculum topics. Works on a classroom projector or individual Chromebooks with no accounts needed.
Students
Practice spelling, expand vocabulary, and build letter-pattern intuition — all while playing a classic game that rewards smart guessing over random clicking. The keyboard shortcut support means students can type letters directly without touching the mouse.
Word game fans
Hangman sharpens the same skills as Wordle and Scrabble: letter frequency awareness, pattern recognition, and risk management under limited guesses. Start with E, T, A, O, I, N — the six highest-frequency letters in English — to reveal the word shape fast.
Parents & families
A screen-time activity that builds real vocabulary skills. The Animals and Food categories work well for younger kids, while General Words and Science give older kids a genuine challenge. No ads, no accounts, no install — just open and play.
