How do I use the Wordle Helper?

Open your Wordle game (NYT, a clone, or anywhere you play Wordle), enter your first guess there, and come back here. Enter the same word in the first row of the table above and color-code each tile by clicking on them, corresponding to the colors provided by Wordle (grey = letter not in word, green = correct position, yellow = in word but wrong position). Then click on "Analyze," and the tool will suggest the best word for your next guess.

If you don't currently have an active Wordle game open, switch to the Practice Mode tab (in the top-right of your browser window). A random Wordle 'word' will be chosen, and your tiles will be auto-colored as the solver arrives at it in order to demonstrate to you how it works.

How does the Wordle Helper pick the best next guess?

This tool uses Shannon entropy (the same as used internally by NYT's WordleBot) to score all candidate words for how much information they would give you. After each guess, I filter the list of candidates down to the subset of words that fit all of your previous clues. For each of the remaining candidates, I score them based on the number of possible outcome patterns that word could generate. The highest-scoring word is your best guess.

Guesses that can generate many different outcome patterns (e.g. 20) are more valuable than ones that can only generate a few (e.g. 3) no matter what, because they will always narrow down the possibilities to the same extent.

In our benchmark of 10,000 simulated games (Python seed 42), the solver solved all games in 6 or fewer moves, on average with 3.02 moves. You can find the complete analysis and comparison table here →

Why doesn't this solver use the original 2,309-word Wordle answer list?

NYT continues to modify the list of words after they purchased the game, thus any answer pool 'solver' currently available will be out of date. The original list of 2,309 words was released publicly while Wordle was an independent game, and many solvers (including myself) immediately memorized it. This solution uses real-world frequency of words in order to rank the best answer, and thus continues to be useful as the game evolves.

What's the best starting word for Wordle?

TRACE is the highest entropy opener (5.989 bits), followed by SLATE (5.986) and then SNARE (5.985). The best openers will test common letters such as E, A, R, I, O, T, N, S in different arrangements without repeating any. CRANE and RAISE are also excellent choices for a starting word. To put any of the letters from the word picker into a particular chip of the starting word, simply click on it. The full list of the 100 best openers and downloadable data can be found at Best Wordle Starting Words.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Wordle Helper work?
Enter your guesses one by one. Tap on the tiles to set the colors of the letters that Wordle has given you (green = correct position, yellow = in word, but wrong position, grey = letter is not in word). After that, click on "Analyze". The tool then tests all of the remaining candidate words to see which one splits the remaining possibilities most evenly. This is also the method NYT's WordleBot uses: measuring Shannon entropy in bits of information. The best word for you is then highlighted as the top result.
Is this the official Wordle solver from the New York Times?
No. This is a completely independent tool, provided for free and with no account creation required. It is in no way affiliated with the New York Times or the Wordle game itself. The paid WordleBot feature for the NYT Word puzzle is a different product from this free tool.
What are the best starting words for Wordle?
Our information-theory analysis of all 100 possible Wordle openers shows that, in terms of information content, the best 3 openers are TRACE (5.989 bits), then SLATE and SNARE (both 5.966 bits). The chart above shows the information content of all 100 openers, and a full ranked list is available as a CSV file as well as a 10,000-game benchmark.
What is Practice Mode?
Practice Mode allows you to play with the Wordle Helper in a practice mode where no current Wordle game has to be open. You start with a random secret word and enter your guesses as usual. The tiles get colorized automatically for you. Additionally, after each guess, a hint panel opens up and suggests the best next guess. Important: the secret word is random and not the same as the Wordle answer of today.
How is this different from other Wordle solvers?
Most Wordle solver tools show a list of words that could possibly solve the game after a series of guesses. Instead, this tool ranks the list of possible words by their Shannon entropy and recommends the single highest entropy word to guess next. The Practice Mode for this tool shows how the solver tool works without having to open a Wordle game to test it.
Is using a Wordle solver cheating?
In essence, this is a Wordle solver. But the tool cannot know the answer to the current Wordle. It only knows the information that the colors of the tiles in the input give it. Thus, it is similar to the New York Times Wordle solver, WordleBot, which players use to analyze their solution after they solve a Wordle and to get advice on how to play better while solving. The line on when to use a Wordle solver is a matter of personal preference. Playing with a Wordle solver is still playing Wordle.
Does the Wordle Helper support colorblind mode?
Yes — you can toggle to colorblind mode by clicking on the "Colorblind mode" button at the top of the page. In this mode, the tiles will be colored blue for correct positions and orange for wrong positions. This setting is saved for the duration of your session.
