About words ending in -URE

The "-ure" suffix marks nouns of action and its results. "Pressure" is what pressing produces, "closure" is what closing achieves, "culture" is the cultivated result of human activity. The suffix also appears in adjectives (mature, pure, obscure) and verbs (ensure, endure, secure), making "-ure" one of English's more flexible endings.

Part of speech: Nouns denoting an action, process, or result (pressure, culture, measure) and some adjectives (pure, obscure, mature). The suffix derives from Latin "-ura."

Using -URE words in writing and word games

"SURE," "CURE," and "PURE" are common short plays. Longer "-ure" words like "CREATURE," "PRESSURE," and "PLEASURE" reward players who can span bonus squares.
