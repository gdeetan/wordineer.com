About Wordineer

Fast, free, and privacy-friendly word tools for parents, students, educators, adults, children, basically anyone who wants to expand their vocabulary or reminisce about old times through word games and the plethora of word tools.

Our Story

Wordineer is a self-funded site consisting of word and language tools I run from my home office. No apps. Nothing to download. No upsells.

How Wordineer Got Its Name

It's a play on words: Word + Engineer. Hence the term "Wordineer." The idea is an engineer who plays with words. So most of the tools you'll see on the website are about words, from word generators to password generators.

Why I Built It

My wife and I homeschool our only son. I needed a resource for him to look up words. Not just words but definitions of those words, because his vocabulary was limited. We're always encouraging him to expand his vocabulary, and that's basically the genesis of this website.

Most of the sites I saw had heavy ad placements that can be distracting. The goal for Wordineer is to have a website not flooded with ads, but still earns enough to support my family without the hassle of signing up.

I know it's a terrible business model, but I saw a need.

What's Actually Behind the Words

Nothing is scraped from other websites, that's a big red flag. Nothing is made up. I try to research as much as possible about what words are valid and what aren't. For example, I was curious about Gen-Z terminology, so I researched what these words are and their exact meaning.

Wordle started to gain popularity in the early 2020s, which is one reason I built the Wordle helper. I manually curate these words for accuracy and train them on a simulation of 10,000 games.

What You'll Find Here

A few of the tools people use most often:

  • Random Word Generator — generates random words, with filters for length, part of speech, and starting letter.
  • Random Name Generator — random names, by region, with meaning.
  • Word of the Day — a fresh definition each morning.
  • Sight Words by grade — Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and up.
  • Spelling Bee Words by grade — printable and searchable.
  • Wordle Helper — powered by the 10,000-game simulation.
  • Word Counter — for essays, blog posts, and social captions.
  • Rhyming Dictionary — for songwriters and poets.
  • Password Generator — with length and character controls. I use this often.
  • Lorem Ipsum Generator — plus dozens of other writing and developer utilities.

The full list lives on the Word Tools hub and the Name Generators hub.
