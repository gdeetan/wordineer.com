8-letter N words for Scrabble and vocabulary building

The medium and hard tiers introduce more nuanced vocabulary. Nepotism names a specific unfair behavior in institutions. Neurosis is the clinical term for excessive anxiety. Nihilism is the philosophy that denies all moral authority. Numinous captures the feeling of an awe-inspiring spiritual presence that no simpler word matches.

Harder N words like nebulous, nescient, and notional expand writing precision considerably. Nebulous describes haziness in thought or form. Notional names something that exists only in theory. Nescient flags ignorance in formal discourse. Each fills a gap that general vocabulary cannot cover as precisely.

Harder N words worth knowing

Neophyte is more precise than beginner — it implies someone new to a specific discipline or belief. Nihilist is not just a pessimist but someone who rejects all moral and social values. Nacreous describes the specific iridescent quality of pearl — far more evocative than shiny. These words reward the effort to learn them.
