How to Play Wordle

In each game, you have to find a hidden 5-letter word. You have 6 tries to guess it. Enter a 5-letter word and press Enter. The tiles change color after each guess. A green tile means a letter is found in the correct position. A yellow tile means a letter is found in the word, but not in the correct position. A grey tile means a letter is not found in the word. You can use this information to guess the next letter.

All of your guesses must be real words. You won't be able to type on the on-screen keyboard until you've entered 5 letters, and it will "shake" if you try to enter an invalid word. The on-screen keyboard keeps track of the letters you have eliminated, confirmed, and moved to new positions. You can easily see this information without having to go back and look through your past guesses. If you get stuck, the Wordle helper narrows the list of possible answers from the letters you already know.

Challenge Mode

This link will contain the secret word that you are currently playing with. The recipient can play the rest of the game cold, not knowing the answer. You can share this link at any time during the game or even after you have completed the game. The word is hidden in the URL hash and therefore not sent to the server and is not visible in most URLs.

You can use this Wordle word generator to find a good secret word to use for your puzzle and then hit the Play button on any result in order to play that word. Then, you can just hit the Share button for that finished puzzle and copy the resulting URL to send to your target to play.

Difficulty Levels

The difficulty dropdown determines how common the word chosen at random is. Easy words are very common, everyday words that most people know straight away. Medium words are less common than Easy words but are generally well known. Hard words are even less common and can include technical or even archaic words. These sort of words occasionally appear in the official Wordle answer list and can be a real puzzle for even experienced players. Changing the difficulty resets the board with a brand new, randomly chosen word of the chosen difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

What do the tile colors mean?
These show up as green letters in the word (below). For the on-screen keyboard above, each letter key shows the best result of that letter in all of your guesses, which may be in a different position in the word below. Yellow letters are in the word but in different positions than shown above. Grey letters are not in the word at all.
How do I challenge a friend?
Click the Share button for the current game at any time (even after you have finished playing it). A URL will be copied to your clipboard that includes the secret word for that game encoded in the link. You can then share this URL with your friends, and they can play the same Wordle word without ever finding out the secret word. As an alternative to creating a challenge from a single Wordle word you can also create a challenge from the random Wordle word generator by clicking the Play icon for any word and then clicking the Share button on the game page for that Wordineer game.
Can I play unlimited games?
Yes. Every time you click Play after a game, you can play a new round with a completely random word. You can even go back to a previous word and try a different difficulty level (e.g., Easy for a word that you found too hard).
What's the best Wordle opening word?
Words like CRANE, SLATE, RAISE, and STARE are also very effective because they use all 5 letters to test for the presence of 5 of the most common letters in the language: E, R, A, T, S, and N. Typically, the best word is one that tests for the greatest amount of coverage of common letters, avoiding words with rare letters and words with repeated letters. See more options in best Wordle starting words.
Does this use the official Wordle word list?
This game uses Wordineer's curated 5-letter word dataset that includes a broad cross-section of the most common words as well as less common words, organized into three levels of difficulty. (This game is not the official New York Times Wordle game. For one, it uses 6 guesses and, more importantly, you must guess a valid English word. Also, the color feedback that you get for each guess is the same as for each guess in the original game.)
