About words ending in -NESS

The "-ness" suffix is one of the simplest nominalisation tools in English: attach it to almost any adjective and you get a noun naming that quality as an abstract state. "Happiness," "sadness," "brightness," "loneliness" — these are all formed this way. Because the pattern is so predictable, encountering an unfamiliar "-ness" word rarely blocks comprehension.

Part of speech: Abstract nouns formed from adjectives. The suffix converts a quality into a state or condition (kind → kindness, dark → darkness, aware → awareness).
