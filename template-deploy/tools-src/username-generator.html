What is a username generator?

The username generator is a tool that helps users generate usernames without cycling through permutations of their name just to find something acceptable for a website. No more guesswork of typing 'yourname_' or 'yourname_1234'; this tool does it for you by leveraging a set of rules and a pool of words.

All of this happens locally in your browser. Nothing is logged on the server, so if you are concerned with data privacy, you've come to the right place. However, since the tools are local, we cannot check the availability of usernames on any given platform - you'll have to do that yourself.

The best way to use this tool is to generate in batches, then try the results on the website you're registering with. Use the heart icon to save usernames you like, and they stay in your browser until you clear the cache.

What makes a good username?

Short usernames are better than long ones. In general, you want to choose one short enough to easily remember and say out loud. Most websites allow usernames of 12 characters or less. Longer usernames can also exist, but a short, unique, easy-to-remember username is better. There are always exceptions, so this can be case-by-case.

Don't substitute in 0 for O's or 1 for l's (e.g., if your username is YourName0, people will try to type in YourName when they hear you say it out loud). These handles look great until someone tries to type out your username and it doesn't work.

The Handle fits best on one particular Platform. For example, on Instagram, the Handle for your gaming Profile might look very different from the one you would use for your Portfolio on Discord. Thus, the generators for Tryhard Handles are especially useful for gamers signing up for new Accounts on Xbox Live, while those for Instagram Handle generators are best for creatives and business owners looking for handles for their new accounts on the Photo- and Video-sharing Platform.

The seed word feature

As for the seed word input, this is a feature that most other username generators are lacking. Instead of only generating a totally random username, our tool will create a lot of usernames that are based on the rule set and style pool you chose, and then also include a lot of words that start with the same letters as your seed word, or words that otherwise sound similar to your seed word.

Note that if there are not enough words in the style pool that start with the same letters as your input word (i.e., seed), that tool will simply fall back to using the full style pool for results. Your input word helps to focus the results of the tool towards results that fit the same general "theme" that you're looking for, rather than just generating a totally random set of results.

How the cross-platform preview works

Below the result for every username is a little cross-platform preview. A pretty Instagram handle might look terrible as a Gmail address. A simple username looks horrible as a Twitter handle. This little strip of HTML helps you avoid those nasty surprises and quickly see whether a username that seems perfect for one platform isn't suitable for another.

You can heart each result to add it to the bottom favorites tray. Once you've generated enough results, you can copy and paste all of your favorites into a note and compare them side by side to see which you like best.

Style guide

As for Cool, this style pulls from the following 'power words': apex, void, rogue, phantom and titan. Very strong, confident, and even a little aggressive, this style suits most gaming platforms and accounts that want to project a strong personality online.

Aesthetic handles belong to eight different subcultures, with separate pools of words. We can suggest Cottagecore (rural and cozy) or Dark Academia (esoteric and gothic), or VSCO girls and Soft Girls (Pastel Sweet), Cyberpunk (of a techno-neon world), Vintage items (with a faded, antique look), Simple Minimalist things (sparse and clean), and Whimsical items of Fairycore (from a magical world).

Cute usernames are generally playful — baba, mochi, pudding, and peach are examples of cute usernames.

This generator can be configured specifically to search for certain word combinations (or puns) or affectionate word combinations with the same ending (or alliteration).

Edgy is another strong word. They are dark and sharp: venom, wraith, obsidian, abyss. That sort of thing is generally best suited to gamers or very creative people. Like with Funny, Edgy can be a tryhard name on the wrong site. So use with caution.

Kawaii uses Japanese words for "cute" in romanized form. Examples include yuki, hana, neko, and usagi. This is often used in anime communities, on gaming servers, and on deeply aesthetic accounts.

The Fantasy pool has 4 Style sub-pools: High Fantasy (mostly elvish words), Dark Fantasy (mostly ominous words), Norse (Viking names), and Celtic (Irish or Scottish words). You can choose a sub-pool to generate words that sound as if they belong to a particular fantasy world.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check if my username is available?
No, the generator cannot perform live availability checks for you. To check if a username is available on a certain platform, you can open each potential username in a new tab. Instagram: instagram.com/[handle], Reddit: reddit.com/u/[handle], etc. Easy, short handles are often taken on larger platforms, whereas longer or more unusual handles are generally available.
Why does the generator show 12 results at once?
People generally scan through a list of usernames rather than evaluating each one individually. Therefore, rather than forcing you to go through each potential username one at a time, this tool starts by generating a list of twelve usernames. You can then go through this list and add your favorites to the bottom of the page in the Favorites area. This allows you to go through as many batches of potential usernames as necessary before making a final decision.
Can I use these usernames on any platform?
The main tool doesn't check for specific character limits and reserved characters for specific platforms, so you may end up generating names that aren't acceptable for a particular site before you realize that. But every platform has its own generator tool, so you can simply switch to the dedicated tool for the site you are about to sign up for and it will always work within the correct character limits and never suggest names containing any forbidden characters.
What does format option do?
Changes the "glue" type between two words. The resulting single word can then be formatted as desired. For example, lowercase gives darkwolf, camelCase gives DarkWolf, underscores give dark_wolf, dots give dark.wolf, etc. Note that different platforms have their own (unstated) conventions here: Instagram handles often use dots or underscores, for example, while Discord handles are typically entered in lowercase, or in camelCase. For gaming identities on Xbox and Steam, camelCase (no spaces, no special characters) is typical.
Are these usernames offensive filtered?
This includes offensive terms. Any generated username that includes such words will be silently removed by the generator. The tool continues to generate more candidates underneath until it has created twelve suitable usernames, even if this means it has to go through as many as 200 candidates in total.
Is my generated data stored anywhere?
None of the data generated or entered is stored. The Favorites you have hearted in the stored set will be lost when you exit the page or when you reload the page. None of the generated names, nor any of the words that you enter into the seed word field, will ever be sent to a server. It all stays within the boundaries of the tab in which it was generated – it never leaves your machine if the name isn't taken out of the tab.
