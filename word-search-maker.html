How to make a word search

  1. Enter your words — type them in the box, one per line, or click a theme button to load a preset list. You can edit preset words freely.
  2. Choose a grid size — 10×10 for short word lists (up to 10 words), 15×15 for standard classroom puzzles, 20×20 for longer lists or harder puzzles.
  3. Pick word directions — horizontal only is easiest (good for grades K–2), all directions is hardest (good for older students and adults).
  4. Click Generate — your puzzle is built instantly. Click Generate again to shuffle the layout and get a different arrangement.
  5. Print or download — use the Print button for a clean, watermark-free printout. Use Download PNG to save an image file to share digitally.

Tips for good word search puzzles

Keep words between 4 and 10 letters for the best balance of challenge and findability. Very short words (2–3 letters) appear accidentally in the filler letters and frustrate solvers. Very long words on a small grid may not place. For a classroom puzzle, 10–15 words on a 15×15 grid is the sweet spot.

Use the answer key toggle to check word placement before printing. Click any word in the list below the grid to highlight just that word — useful for checking or for hinting to a stuck student without revealing everything.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a word search?

Type your words into the box (one per line), choose a grid size and word direction, then click Generate. Your puzzle is ready instantly — no account, no download needed. Click Print for a clean, printable version.

Can I print without a watermark?

Yes — completely free, no watermarks, no sign-up. Click the Print button and your browser's print dialog opens showing just the puzzle grid and word list. Save it as a PDF or print directly to paper.

Why are some of my words missing from the puzzle?

Words longer than the grid size can't be placed. On a 10×10 grid, words with more than 10 letters won't fit. Switch to a 15×15 or 20×20 grid, or shorten the word. Unplaced words are shown with a dashed border in the word list.

Does this work on Chromebooks and tablets?

Yes. The generator runs entirely in the browser — no app, no plugin, no installation. It works on Chromebooks, iPads, Android tablets, and any desktop browser. The puzzle generates and prints from any of these devices.

Who uses the Word Search Maker?

Teachers
Create vocabulary review puzzles tied to the week's spelling list, a science unit, or a reading passage. Generate a fresh puzzle in under a minute, print a class set, and use the answer key toggle on the projector to review answers.
Parents
Make a themed puzzle for a road trip, rainy afternoon, or holiday activity. The food, animals, and holidays presets are ready in one click — or enter names from a birthday guest list for a personalized activity.
Event planners
Wedding word searches with the couple's names and meaningful words, baby shower puzzles with nursery theme words, office party games with company or team names. Download as PNG to include in digital invitations or slide decks.
Homeschoolers
Build puzzles around any subject — history vocabulary, science terms, foreign language words. Change the direction setting to match the child's level: horizontal only for beginners, all directions for more advanced learners.
