How to play Mad Libs

Mad Libs is a classic word game where the humor comes from filling in blanks without knowing the story first.

  1. Manual mode (best for groups): One person is the "reader." They see the blank labels — like "Adjective" or "Person's Name" — and ask the group for words without revealing the story. Fill in each blank, then click Reveal Story and read it aloud.
  2. Random mode (best for solo play): Click Random Story and the generator fills all blanks automatically from its word database. Great for a quick laugh or for getting ideas before playing with a group.
  3. Share your result: After the story is revealed, click Share Link to copy a URL. Anyone who opens it sees the exact same completed story.

Tips for better games

In Manual mode, have players shout out words without thinking too hard — the funnier and more random, the better. Try the Holiday category around Thanksgiving or Christmas. The Funny stories work great as icebreakers at parties or team meetings.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mad Libs?

Mad Libs is a word game where one player asks others to provide words — a noun, a verb, an adjective — without revealing the story. When all blanks are filled, the story is read aloud, usually with hilarious results. It was invented in 1953 and has sold over 110 million books.

Can I play Mad Libs alone?

Yes — switch to Random mode. One click fills all blanks automatically and reveals the story instantly. You can keep clicking for a new random result until you find one that makes you laugh.

Is this free? Do I need an account?

Completely free, forever. No account, no sign-up, no app download. Works in any browser on any device.

Can I share my completed Mad Lib with friends?

Yes — after the story reveals, click Share Link. It copies a URL to your clipboard that opens the exact same completed story for anyone you send it to.

What age is Mad Libs good for?

Ages 6 and up. Younger kids learn parts of speech in a fun context. The Kids and Holiday categories are ideal for family play. Funny, Adventure, and Sports stories work well for teens and adults.
