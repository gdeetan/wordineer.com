Frequently asked questions

How many 5-letter words start with CL?

This list has 67 five-letter words starting with CL that have definitions. 25 are common everyday words. Wordle's answer list includes 13 of them.

What are the best CL words for Wordle?

Clean first. Clone if you already know O is in the word. Close is a frequent answer but it repeats E and does not tell you as much as clean.

Clean used my E and A. What is left?

Look at cling, clink, and clock — they keep CL and swap in I or O. If L appears twice (as in cool-style answers), this list’s double-L words are worth a scan.
