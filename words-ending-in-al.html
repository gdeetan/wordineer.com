About words ending in -AL

The "-al" suffix is one of the most common in English, converting nouns into adjectives of relationship and belonging. "Natural" relates to nature, "digital" relates to digits, "musical" relates to music. Because the pattern is so regular, it enables rapid vocabulary expansion — recognising the suffix often lets you infer the adjective from the noun it derives from.

Part of speech: Mostly adjectives meaning "of," "relating to," or "characterized by" the base noun (natural, digital, musical). A smaller group are nouns (animal, capital, arrival).

Using -AL words in writing and word games

"AL" adjectives are abundant in technical and formal registers. Medical, legal, scientific, and cultural vocabulary all lean heavily on "-al" forms, which is why advanced reading comprehension often hinges on mastering this suffix.
