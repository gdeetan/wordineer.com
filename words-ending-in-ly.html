About words ending in -LY

Adding "-ly" to an adjective is one of the most regular patterns in English. It produces adverbs that modify verbs, other adjectives, or whole clauses. Partly because the transformation is so transparent, "-ly" adverbs are a core tool for precise, varied writing — they let you say how, when, or to what degree without restructuring a sentence.

Part of speech: The vast majority are adverbs formed by adding -ly to an adjective (quick → quickly). A smaller set are adjectives themselves (friendly, lively, lonely) or nouns (family, rally).

Using -LY words in writing and word games

"LY" is a useful two-letter play in Scrabble and Words With Friends, but its real value comes from the long words it unlocks. Words like "quickly," "closely," and "strongly" score well and are easy to remember because their base adjective is already familiar.
