Frequently asked questions

How many 5-letter words start with BR?

This list has 71 five-letter words starting with BR that have definitions. 30 are common everyday words. Wordle's answer list includes 10 of them.

What are the best BR words for Wordle?

Brief first. Brave if A is still unknown and you want a likely answer. Break is common but it repeats nothing useful after brave.

Brave showed A yellow. Next?

Move the A and try break or brand-style tails. If R is also yellow (not green), the second R may sit later — bread/broad patterns. Use the helper if more than one yellow is bouncing.
